# Implementation Plan: VirtualTable MVVM Command & Data Binding Architecture

**Branch**: `009-virtual-table-mvvm-binding` | **Date**: 2026-10-21 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/009-virtual-table-mvvm-binding/spec.md`

---

## Summary

Refactor `VirtualTable` to expose standard WinUI 3 `ICommand` DependencyProperties (`SortCommand`, `CellEditedCommand`) and extract the embedded data adapter (`TableDataPageAdapter`) out of `DataViewControl.xaml.cs` into the ViewModel/Model layer. This enables pure declarative XAML binding (`{x:Bind}`) without code-behind event handlers, reduces `DataViewControl.xaml.cs` from 208 lines to under 35 lines, preserves backward-compatible .NET events, and prepares `VirtualTable` for standalone library distribution.
